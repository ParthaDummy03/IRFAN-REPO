# TAZA FOOD CORNER — UI design mapping

## Visual system

- Brand text: **TAZA FOOD CORNER**
- Palette: **Red `#D71920`, black `#101010`, white `#FFFFFF`**, plus neutral grey tones for borders and secondary text.
- Theme: a white mode and dark mode; both retain the red accent and use only black/white/grey surfaces.
- Style: bold editorial headings, uppercase micro-labels, outlined controls, square cards, red action buttons.
- Responsive: desktop, tablet and mobile breakpoints.

## Page 1 — Home

Header: Logo, EN/Bangla label toggle, white/dark theme toggle, cart, menu drawer, sign-in/account. Main navigation includes Home, Menu, Offers and Dine-in.

Main content: hero copy and two calls to action (Delivery and Dine-in); two route cards; three featured offer banners; featured food cards; brand statement.

Menu drawer: Offers, Coupons, Order history, Delivery, Find an outlet, Outlet opening hours, Feedback, FAQ, Contact us, About, Sign in / Sign out.

Footer: Outlet opening hour, Feedback, FAQ, Contact us and About.

## Page 2 — Menu / all products

Shows the full active menu, total visible item count, category filter chips, search field, product unit label, price, quantity input, and add-to-cart action. Cart can update or remove quantities.

## Page 3 — Outlet finder

Explains location permission first. The browser permission prompt appears only after the visitor clicks **Use my location**. Radius range updates the nearby-outlets REST request. The map marks each result; the adjacent list shows address, phone, opening hours, distance (when available), demo status and a Select action. If the visitor declines location access, manual outlet selection still works.

## Page 4 — Confirmed order

Displays an order number, confirmation state, delivery/dine-in type, order status, payment status, order line items, subtotal, delivery fee, discount and total. It provides a link to the payment page.

## Page 5 — Payment option

Displays cash on delivery / cash at outlet, plus explicitly marked demo bKash/card options. No banking credentials or card data are requested or saved.

## Business and data-management pages

The side drawer, footer and admin panel make the additional features available: offers, coupons, order history, outlet hours, feedback, FAQ, contact, about, staff order management, delivery status management and content administration.
