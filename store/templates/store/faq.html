Everything you need to know before placing your Taza order.

How do I place a delivery order?

Choose Delivery on the home page, browse the menu, add items to your cart and complete the checkout form with your phone number and delivery address.

How do I find a dine-in outlet?

Choose Dine-in, press “Use my location” to request browser location permission, set your search radius and select an outlet from the map or list.

Can I update the outlet locations and phone numbers?

Yes. Sign in to the Django Admin panel and update the Outlet records. The locations in the starter data are clearly marked as demo locations.

Are online bKash and card payments real?

No. They are demonstration-only options in this student project. They do not charge money or connect to a payment provider. Cash on delivery and pay-at-outlet are the available real-world workflow choices in this sample.

Where can I see my previous orders?

Create an account and sign in. Open the menu and choose Order history to view orders placed while signed in.

What if I do not allow location access?

You can still see the outlet list and select an outlet manually. Distance filtering will be available only after you share your location.
